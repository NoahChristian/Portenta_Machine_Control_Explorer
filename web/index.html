Readings are raw ADC counts at the MCU pad, not engineering units. Each channel's 0–10 V / 4–20 mA / NTC mode is a four-bit GPIO switch network that nothing drives yet, so the front end is in an undefined mode and these numbers say nothing about any signal on the terminal block. What they do show is that the ADC path works and that the SYSCFG analog switches are open.
