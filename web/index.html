J and K are the same chip. The datasheet gives one MAX31855KASA+T for thermocouples and describes J as that part “with software multiplication coefficient”, so J is an approximation computed from a K-type measurement, not a second front end. The coefficient scales the difference from the cold junction rather than the reported temperature — scaling the reading itself is wrong by roughly the cold-junction temperature, about 5 °C in a warm room, which is small enough to pass for calibration. The ratio is derived from the Seebeck coefficients and has never been checked against a J probe here, which is why the raw K reading is kept in the table beside it.

The board above is drawn from tools/terminals.csv, not traced from a photograph, so it carries no license obligation and every terminal is a live element rather than a picture of one. Terminal order comes from Arduino's published pinout; nothing on it has been confirmed at the connector yet, which is why every channel is dashed. Readings are raw ADC counts at the MCU pad, not engineering units: each channel's 0–10 V / 4–20 mA / NTC mode is a four-bit GPIO switch network that nothing drives yet, so the front end is in an undefined mode. What the numbers do show is that the ADC path works and that the SYSCFG analog switches are open.
